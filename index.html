      What matters most

      Set a low and high you'd believe for each assumption. Each bar shows how far SOM moves across that range with everything else held at base. The longest bar is the number worth researching first. Faded rows use the default range.

      How it works

      TAM is everyone who could ever buy, times what they would pay in a year. SAM is the slice your product, channels and geography can actually serve. SOM is the share of SAM you can realistically win in the near term.

      Top-down starts broad and narrows. Bottom-up counts what your go-to-market can actually reach and convert. If the two land within 2x of each other, the estimate is probably reasonable. If not, one set of assumptions needs another look.

      Example numbers are illustrative assumptions for demonstration, not researched figures.
